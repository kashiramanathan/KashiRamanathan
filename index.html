Safety io / MSA Safety · 2019–2025

AI-Powered Maintenance Intelligence for Fire Services

Unstructured Maintenance Log Classification · LLM + RAG · Trust Before Automation

The Real Problem

Safety signals were lost in operational noise. Technician logs, inspection notes, and maintenance records existed as unstructured free text. Critical asset issues were detected late — or not at all — because rules-based logic could not triage the volume and variability of real-world operational language.

Product North Star

Trust before automation. The goal was not to replace human judgment but to surface explainable, auditable risk signals early enough for experts to act. Every AI output needed clear provenance, human oversight gates, and acceptance criteria grounded in safety regulations and historical incident severity.

Technical Strategy & Approach

  • Designed an LLM + Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system that converted unstructured technician logs into structured asset-risk signals.
  • Grounded outputs in safety regulations (NFPA-style), historical incident data, and internal severity rules to ensure explainability and auditability.
  • Defined model evaluation frameworks and production acceptance criteria focused on real-world performance, not just lab accuracy.
  • Implemented structured prompt engineering and iterative testing to reduce classification variance.
  • Partnered with Engineering and Data on MLOps practices: monitoring, version control, and continuous performance iteration.

Ruthless Prioritization

Explicitly said no to full predictive maintenance in the first phase. Focused instead on high-volume, high-clarity triage of unstructured logs where trust could be earned quickly and measurement was unambiguous. Expanded scope only after the system demonstrated reliable behavior under real operational load.

Independent AI Product Consulting · 2026–Present

Centralized AI Orchestration for Multi-Domain SaaS

Single Front Door Architecture · Model Routing · Shadow AI → Governed Platform

The Problem

Fragmented “Shadow AI” experimentation at scale. Domain teams were adopting LLMs independently, creating uncontrolled cost, inconsistent governance, PII exposure risk, and duplicated effort. Leadership needed a path from experimentation to a secure, centralized, commercially accountable architecture.

The Solution

Designed a domain-agnostic “Single Front Door” AI orchestration platform that centralizes PII masking, programmatic governance, and intelligent model routing across teams while preserving domain autonomy for innovation.

Key Product Decisions

  • Model-agnostic routing framework balancing frontier LLMs and targeted smaller models (SLMs).
  • Centralized semantic caching and policy enforcement to control token consumption and compliance posture.
  • Phased pilot rollout with internal product and care teams, creating reusable platform frameworks.
  • Clear commercial framing: OPEX savings and time-to-value as primary success metrics.
